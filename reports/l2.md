## What changed, in short

- **Returning (C5).** World.tsx keeps a `returning` state. Whenever the tour or free roam hands back to the page at full motion, the page stays hidden and inert, with `html[data-world-mode='returning']`. Any one of these ends it:
  - `onFlight('approach' | 'end')` for the page's station;
  - no flight to the station has started by the third rAF (the camera was already there), or one is already on approach;
  - 6.5s pass.

  Below full motion the page comes back at once. When it does come back, the page fades in with a 600ms Web Animation on main, header and footer: a transition would override the header's and footer's own. World.scss hides the page and the veil in `returning` as it does for the tour, plus `.header-scrim`.
- **No ghost of a hidden page (FX09).**
  - Visit goes through `navigateFromMode`.
  - `worldIsLive()` (pageSnapshot) now also needs `html[data-world-mode] === 'page'`, so `snapshotPage` bails while a mode or `returning` hides the page.
  - PageTransition decides whether to hold the copy with a new `worldOnScreen()`, which ignores the mode. A page opened from a mode (Visit, docking) also waits for the camera.
- **Tour (E6).** State in `worldMode`:
  - `WorldModeState` gains `tourStep`, the number of stops shown so far: 0..n, and n is the closing card. `tourStop` is still the station's index in `tourStops`, so WorldCanvas did not change.
  - `startTour(from?)` defaults to the station of the current path (index 0 when it isn't navigable). `advanceTour()` goes on to the closing card after the last stop, and `backTour()` steps back.
  - `launchWorldMode` now calls `startTour()` with no argument.

  The TourOverlay card:
  - Two new round controls: Previous stop, and Pause, with `aria-pressed` and the accessible name "Pause the tour".
  - Keys: Space on the card itself toggles pause, ←/→ step, and neither reacts inside inputs or dialogs.
  - Dwell is `clamp(3500 + 45·text length, 5500, 10000)` ms. It drives `--tour-dwell` on the card, which runs the fill bar.
  - Pausing shows "· Paused" in the step line, inside the live region. The countdown also pauses through a MutationObserver on `data-paused`.
  - Captions on touch: click/hover → tap (`(hover: none), (pointer: coarse)`).
  - A stop lands at once below full motion, and also when no flight there has started by the third rAF.
  - Closing card: Open Contact (hidden on /contact), Fly freely from here (`launchWorldMode('explore')`, so the pointer lock happens inside the click), and Back to <page>.
  - The card takes focus when the tour starts, and again whenever the control that had focus goes away.
  - The card is now up to 620px wide.
- **Showcase (E5).** `land()` calls `showcase(station, 'tour')` 600ms later, at full motion only.
- **Focus (G1, FX16).** `worldMode.set()` remembers `document.activeElement` when a mode starts from `page`. `arrivedAt` marks the return as a new page. `restoreFocus()` focuses the remembered element, else `.roam-fab`, else `#main-content`, using `preventScroll` and skipping anything inert, hidden or disconnected. On a new page it focuses `#main-content`, setting `tabIndex = -1` if the markup has none. World.tsx calls it in a rAF once `hidden` turns false.

## Doc notes (for AGENTS.md / CLAUDE.md)

- **Modes.** `html[data-world-mode]` gains `'returning'`. At full motion, after the tour or free roam hands back, the page stays hidden and inert, with the veil off, until one of these, then it fades back in over 600ms (a Web Animation):
  - the flight to its station is on approach or ends;
  - no flight there has started by the third frame;
  - 6.5s pass.

  Below full motion it comes straight back. CSS and code that ask "is the page showing?" should test `html[data-world-mode='page']`, not the store's mode. HeaderHud already treats anything but `page` as away.
- **Page snapshot.** `snapshotPage` bails unless `html[data-world-mode]` is `page`, because the copy carries no id: the rule hiding `#main-content` doesn't hide it. This was the bug behind FX09: Visit left the tour first, then navigated, and the hidden page flashed up and flew off.
- **PageTransition.** It holds the copy whenever the world is on screen (`worldOnScreen`), whatever the mode, so pages opened from a mode wait for the camera.
  - On approach the copy arrives the way the camera flew: from the side after an about-turn (x −sign·8vw, rotateY sign·7deg, perspective 1400px), or rushing up (scale 0.94, y 16) after an ahead flight.
  - The wrapper always settles at `transform: none` and `filter: none`. `transitionEnd` drops the perspective, because a leftover `perspective()` would make the wrapper the containing block for the page's fixed elements.
- **Tour.**
  - `worldMode.startTour(from?)` starts at the current station.
  - `tourStop` is the station's index, and `tourStep` counts the stops shown; `tourStep === tourStops.length` is the closing card.
  - `advanceTour()` and `backTour()` step through the stops.
  - The card has Previous stop and Pause (`aria-pressed`). Space on the card pauses, ←/→ step, and Esc exits.
  - Dwell is `clamp(3500 + 45·caption chars, 5500, 10000)` ms, exposed as `--tour-dwell`.
  - It lands at once below full motion, or when no flight starts by the third frame. At full motion it calls `showcase(station, 'tour')` 600ms after landing.
  - Captions say tap instead of click or hover on touch screens.
  - Closing card: Open Contact, Fly freely from here, Back to <page>.
  - Tests start the tour from the palette (`fill('guided tour')`).
- **Focus.** `worldMode` remembers the focused element when a mode starts from the page. `restoreFocus()` runs one frame after the page stops being inert and tries, in order:
  - the remembered element;
  - `.roam-fab`;
  - `#main-content`.

  After `arrivedAt` (Visit, docking) focus goes to `#main-content`.
- **Tab order.** `<RoamButton />` sits right after `<Header>`, within 15 tab stops on `/`. `.skip-link` is inert while the page is hidden.
- **Cruise.** `html[data-flight='cruise']` (World.tsx) is set for page-mode flights longer than 1.6s after a navigation. The boot warp-in never counts, because its copy arrives as the warp starts. It clears on approach or end. The veil is at 0 while it is set.
- **Veil (FX03).** Dark theme, ≥900px: 70% → 45% at 45% → 0 at 70% of the width. `.page-sub` and `.hero__tag` get a `--bg-primary` halo. The bug: the subtitle measured 3.0:1 at its worst 10% over the nebula's core on /experience.
- **World off (N7).** Under `html:not([data-world-expected])` or `html[data-world='off']`, the backdrop carries the loading screen's star tiles (`app/_stars.scss`) and a still nebula per theme. /skills and project pages use the terminal render. RoamButton reads "Turn on 3D" when the visitor has switched the world off, and only turns it on.

## Risks for reviewers

- **`returning` timing.** If no flight-back events come, the page stays hidden for up to 6.5s. The 3-frame check covers "already there". Real browsers reach the approach long before the cap. SwiftShader in CI flies far slower than real time, because each frame advances at most 1/20s; the e2e tests pass anyway, since most run below full motion and the full-motion ones wait with generous timeouts.
- **The arrival variants were only checked with a patched chunk** (forced reveal), not reached by real approaches. In SwiftShader every reveal came from the 6.5s cap. The swing's direction (copy enters from the side the camera turns towards, opposite the outgoing ghost) is worked out from `flight.turn`'s documented sign: worth a look on real hardware.
- **PageTransition holds the copy in any mode.** If something navigates in free roam without `navigateFromMode` (the browser's Back button), the copy is held for up to 6.5s. The page is hidden by explore mode anyway.
- **The tour card takes focus on start and when its buttons change.** The "Take the tour" button inside main becomes inert, so focus has to go somewhere. The card is a polite live region, as before.
- **`iconify-bundle.json` is a generated file** that other lanes regenerate too. After merging, re-run `node scripts/generate-iconify-bundle.mjs` rather than hand-merging it.
- **Performance.**
  - No new passes and no full-screen geometry.
  - The world-off sky is about 37 gradient layers on one fixed element, painted once (static), only with the world off.
  - The `returning` and tour checks are one-shot rAFs (3 frames).

  - then `'in'` (300ms), cleared after;
  - the rig invalidates at 170ms and 480ms.

  The `World.scss` rules use `!important` over the global reduced-motion rule, which shortens all transitions to 0.01ms. A fade is reduced-motion safe. Add `data-world-cut` to CLAUDE.md's list of debugging attributes.
- **FX04:** the boot warp-in is a flight like any other: `start`, `approach` at 0.6, `end`.

**Projects ride (B15, as reworked in the review)**

`stepRide` and `createRide` live in `stations.ts`. CameraRig keeps one ride and hands `rideProjectFocus(value, angle)` over every frame.

- **Pass-through:** the ride follows the page's settled focus straight through while that moves under half a screen per 1/60s. The threshold is scaled by frame time; per frame alone, 120Hz glides never hopped.
- **Hops:** otherwise it hops. Focus and angle move together, and the angle takes the short way round, so a hop turns at most 180°. Duration is `clamp(0.8 + 0.12·|Δ|, 0.8, 2.2)`s, longer if the peak turn would pass 2.4 rad/s.
  - Bug: hops followed the helix's winding, so the full helix spun the view 1.7 turns at up to 780°/s and passed through screens.
- **A goal that moves mid-hop:**
  - While the hop is setting off, a goal that races on or drifts re-aims the same hop, re-based so it carries on from where it is, and timed for its current goal (never so short it would already be past halfway).
    - Bug: restarting from rest on each frame of a Lenis glide stalled the camera.
  - A real change of course sets off afresh at the current speed (a quintic carry term).
- **Cues:** one `select` when a ride first spans a screen, one `hud-lock` when it lands.
  - Bug: 8-12 `select`s per index click.
- **Reduced motion:** cuts below full motion.
- **Checks to add to CLAUDE.md's camera rule:** for any change to the ride, check:
  - turn rate;
  - total rotation;
  - clearance to every screen (not just the spine);
  - for 1-, 3-, 7- and 14-screen hops, index glides, End and the pager's wrap;
  - at 60 and 120Hz.

Each panel's pull fades towards -1 over the next 20%, and the value is the right-most faded edge. It follows the scroll, so the station eases in and out at the head and foot of a list, and ties between cards side by side are settled. It was fixed at the measure, not by easing over time in `stations.ts`, which would put per-frame state into a pure pose function and turn the still-mode cut into an animation.
- **Simulation** (`fix/c9gap.ts`, the lane's measured layouts):
